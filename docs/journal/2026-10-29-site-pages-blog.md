# Pages and blog for a person

The constructor dropdown mixed four kinds, and the home page was not one of them. Pages and the blog were lists.

## What changed

Страницы creates a page and publishes it. The home page is the page with slug `home`. Блог creates a category, then a post in that category, and publishes either. Конструктор still adds the widgets. The option that was «Шаблон» is labeled as a shared block: it is not a public page.

Each tab posts to its own route, so the right checked is that tab's edit right.

## Why

A person should manage pages and the blog without asking the agent, and the words on the form should match what those records are.
