# The front page has no address

The pages form asked for a slug and called the front page `home`. A wrong address failed the browser check and the click did nothing visible.

## What changed

Страницы starts with «Главная». That record is the only one with `is_home`. It has no public slug. `GET /p/site/` and `GET /p/site/view` render it once it is published. An ordinary page still has an address such as `o-nas`. The form writes the error on the tab. If the panel bridge does not answer, the text says to reload with Ctrl+F5.

## Why

The front page is the domain itself. A person should pick it from the list and see why a save failed.
