# The constructor tab is a dark canvas

The constructor tab was a white form with one text field. The Syntet builder is a dark page: top bar, nested frames, properties on the right.

## What changed

The tab now uses that layout. Blocks, rows and columns stay as a tree and save as the record layout. The properties panel edits the selected widget. Publish saves and marks the record published. The Syntet widget editors (miniatures, AI, device presets, drag) are not in this screen.

## Why

The person was looking at a form and expecting the builder they already use.
